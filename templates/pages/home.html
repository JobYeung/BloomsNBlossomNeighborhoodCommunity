Blooms and Blossom Neighborhood Community (BBNC)

At BBNC, we strive to connect and uplift our community by empowering low-income individuals, the deaf, and homemakers through the art of flower arrangement and charitable contributions.

Our Vision

  1. Showcase Our Creations.
  2. Build a Supportive Marketplace.
  3. Nurture Our Neighbors.

Our Mission: We aim to foster connections among community members, especially for the deaf, by providing opportunities to learn and create through flower arrangement.
